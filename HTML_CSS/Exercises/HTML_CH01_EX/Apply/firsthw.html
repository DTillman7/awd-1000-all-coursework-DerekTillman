To make a good website, there are some things you need to consider. These include design, usability, and content.

When it comes to designing your website, you should focus on creating an appealing layout with consistent colors and typography. Also, consider having your website organized so that users can easily find the information they are looking for.

For usability, it is also important to make sure the website is easy to navigate and user-friendly. You also don't want your website to have any type of delay, such as slow loading times.


For content, it should be relevant, accurate, and engaging to keep the audience interested. You can also find either photos or links to enhance the content.


In Chapter 1, I learned a lot about the internet, including how websites are structured, how to create them effectively, and the importance of design, usability, and content.
