Bowling Ball CoverStocks

A Coverstock is the outermost shell that makes direct contact with the lane surface, and it is the single most important factor in determining how the ball grips the oil and hooks or if you want a straight ball. When you find your bowling style, choosing the right coverstock can make a significant difference in your performance. Bowling ball coverstocks are generally divided into four primary categories, ranging from the least aggressive to the highest performance: plastic, urethane, pearl, and hybrid.


Plastic

Plastic balls are designed to provide a straight shot and are ideal for beginners or players who prefer a more controlled ball motion. Examples include the T Zone Brunswick and house balls.


Urethane

Urethane balls offer more hook potential than plastic balls and are suitable for players who want to add some curve to their shots. They provide a smoother and more controlled reaction on the lane. An example of one is the Purple Hammer by Hammer.


Pearl

Pearl balls are known for their distinctive appearance and are often used by advanced players. They offer a balance between hook potential and control, making them versatile for various lane conditions. Example of one is the Zero Mercy by Storm.

Hybrid

Hybrid balls combine the characteristics of different coverstocks, offering a unique performance profile. They are designed to provide excellent hook potential while maintaining control. Example of one is the Snap Shot by Radical.
